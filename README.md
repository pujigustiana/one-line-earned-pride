# One Line of Earned Pride

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557`.

`main.ts` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet, earned moment
of pride for a baker in a concrete scene — at midnight she pulls her
first loaf from an oven she built herself from scrap, and the loaf
sings as its crust crackles.

The pride is earned inside the line: the oven she built from scrap is
a long effort finally landing, and no one congratulates her or explains
it. The pride arrives at the last word.
